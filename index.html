About

Built by someone who actually gives a damn.

SmartCore Technology was founded by Finley Hassall — someone who’s been obsessed with building things since the age of six. Not in a casual, hobbyist way. In a "this should work properly and not waste anyone’s time" kind of way.

Every system in SmartCore has been designed and built by hand — no off-the-shelf templates, no bloated frameworks. Each feature, workflow, and interface decision has been made with real workplaces in mind. Clarity first. Reliability always.

The company was developed with guidance from Finley’s mother, a business owner herself, which means SmartCore has been shaped by people who understand what it actually means to run something day to day — not just theorise about it.

What SmartCore is built on

  • → Every module solves a real, specific problem
  • → Simple enough that anyone can use it on day one
  • → Clear audit trails and records, always
  • → Scales sensibly as your organisation grows
  • → Built to last — not rebuilt every six months
  • → Works quietly in the background, out of your way
