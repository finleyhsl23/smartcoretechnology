Account & Getting Started
Visit smartcoretechnology.co.uk/shop, choose the modules that suit your business, and complete checkout. Your account is created automatically when you sign up. You'll receive a welcome email with login details.
Go to the login screen at /modules and click Forgot password. Enter your email address and we'll send you a reset link. If you don't receive it within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact support@smartcoretechnology.co.uk.
Once you're logged in, head to SmartCore Core and navigate to the Employees section. You can add employees individually or import them in bulk. Each employee gets their own login linked to your company account.
Company details can be updated in your account settings within SmartCore Core. If you need to change your billing email or something isn't updating correctly, email us at support@smartcoretechnology.co.uk.
Billing & Subscriptions
Pricing depends on which modules you need and the size of your team. Visit our shop to see current pricing and build your plan. Prices are shown live based on your company size and selected modules.
Both options are available. Monthly billing gives you flexibility, while annual billing is paid upfront for the full year. You can choose your preference at checkout.
You can cancel at any time via /cancel-subscriptions or through Manage Plans. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. No questions asked.
We don't offer refunds for partial months or unused time on a monthly plan. If you cancel an annual subscription within 14 days of purchase and haven't used the Service significantly, get in touch at support@smartcoretechnology.co.uk and we'll consider a pro-rata refund.
Visit /manage-plans to add or remove modules from your existing subscription. Changes take effect immediately. Alternatively, head to the shop to explore all available modules.
Invoices are emailed to your registered billing email after each payment. If you need a copy or can't find one, email support@smartcoretechnology.co.uk with your account email and we'll resend it.
Modules & Features
SmartCore currently offers: SmartCore Core (HR & employee management), CRM Enterprise (client management, pipeline, projects), Flexi (bookings & client portal), Presence & Fire Safety, SiteSnap (site inspections), Convoy (fleet management), Holiday Management, and Nova AI Assistant. We also build custom modules tailored to your business — get in touch to find out more.
SmartCore CRM is a full client relationship management system built for field-based and service businesses. It includes client records, a sales pipeline, quoting, project tracking, messaging, and documents — all in one place. It's designed to work alongside your other SmartCore modules.
Flexi is SmartCore's module for fitness studios, personal trainers, and service businesses that manage clients directly. It includes bookings, training plans, client progress tracking, and a mobile-first client portal — so your clients can book and log their progress without downloading an app.
SiteSnap is SmartCore's digital site inspection tool. It lets your team complete audit and inspection forms on the go, capture photo evidence, and generate instant reports. It's built for construction, facilities, and field service businesses.
Convoy is SmartCore's fleet management module. It covers vehicle checks, driver logs, defect reporting, and maintenance records — helping you keep your vehicles roadworthy and your drivers compliant.
Yes. We build custom modules tailored to specific business needs — we've already built bespoke systems for a number of clients. To discuss what you need, email support@smartcoretechnology.co.uk or submit a custom module request from the shop.
