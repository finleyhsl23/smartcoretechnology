Add to Home Screen

No download required. Add SmartCore to your home screen and it opens full screen, just like a native app — works on any device.

  1. 1
    Open Safari
    Visit smartcoretechnology.co.uk/modules/ in Safari. This must be Safari — Chrome and Firefox on iOS can't add web apps.
  2. 2
    Tap Share
    Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen — the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. 3
    Add to Home Screen
    Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add in the top right corner.
  4. 4
    Open from your home screen
    The SmartCore icon will appear on your home screen. Tap it to open full screen with no browser bar.
  1. 1
    Open Chrome
    Visit smartcoretechnology.co.uk/modules/ in Chrome on your Android device.
  2. 2
    Open the menu
    Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top right corner of Chrome.
  3. 3
    Add to Home Screen
    Tap Add to Home Screen. Chrome may also show an Install app banner at the bottom — either works.
  4. 4
    Open from your home screen
    SmartCore will appear on your home screen and open full screen, just like a native app.
